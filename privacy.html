Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

JacekUploader ("the App") is a personal-use tool built to upload and manage video content on the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok API.

What data we access

The App accesses only the TikTok account data necessary to authenticate (via TikTok OAuth) and to publish video content on behalf of the authorizing account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not store TikTok video content beyond what is required to complete an upload.

Data retention

OAuth tokens are stored only as long as needed to operate the App and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account settings.
